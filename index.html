How to play

  • Select one of your ships to see where it can move (green area) and where it can hit (red area).
  • Click inside the green area to move, or click a highlighted enemy to attack.
  • Hold stars with Guards, Lancers, Prisms or Novas to charge the Doomstar. At full charge, move one of them into the Doomstar and press Fire Doomstar.
  • Click an enemy ship to inspect it.
